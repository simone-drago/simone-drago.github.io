My research bridges reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and multi-objective reinforcement learning: learning from people's comparisons when outcomes trade off several objectives and some pairs can't be ranked. I work on it at the Artificial Intelligence and Robotics Laboratory of Politecnico di Milano, from theoretical guarantees to algorithms.

Before my Ph.D., I worked on applied machine learning in industrial projects, including a UAV trajectory planner at ML cube that cut planning time from hours to seconds.
